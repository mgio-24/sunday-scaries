      About this site

      Power rankings weigh the last three weeks of scoring (35%), the last three weeks of best possible lineups (20%), season all-play (25%) and season points per game (20%). They change once a week, after the week is final. Playoff odds come from simulating the rest of the schedule 10,000 times, seeded by record with total points as the tiebreaker. Top six make it, and the top two skip the first round.

      Disputes go to the commissioner. He will not be taking questions.
