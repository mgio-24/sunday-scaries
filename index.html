Power rankings weigh the last three weeks of scoring (35%), the last three weeks of best possible lineups (20%), season all-play (25%) and season points per game (20%). They change once a week, after the week is final. Playoff odds come from simulating the rest of the schedule 10,000 times, seeded by record with total points as the tiebreaker. Top 6 make it and the top 2 get byes.
